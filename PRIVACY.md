# Privacy policy for Local Notetaker

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Local Notetaker is a meeting notetaker for Windows that works entirely on your PC.

## What it uses

- **Your microphone and the sound your PC plays**, while you're taking notes, to transcribe your meeting. You start and stop this, and the app shows when it's listening.
- **Which apps are using your microphone, and the titles of open windows**, to notice when a Microsoft Teams, Google Meet or Zoom meeting starts and ends.
- **The live captions a meeting app shows on screen**, read the way a screen reader reads them, to put people's names on the voices. This is only while you're taking notes, and only if you leave "Put names on voices" on in Settings.

## What it keeps

- **Meeting notes**: Markdown files in the folder you choose, on your PC.
- **A copy of the meeting in progress**: while you're taking notes, kept in the app's own folder on your PC, so a crash or power cut doesn't lose the meeting. It's removed once the notes are saved.
- **Settings and logs**: in the app's own folder on your PC. The logs record what the app did, such as a meeting starting or a voice being matched to a name from the captions. They don't contain what was said.

## What it shares

Nothing. Audio, transcripts, names and settings never leave your PC. The speech engine and its models run on your PC and come with the app. The app has no accounts, sends nothing to its maker or anyone else, and has no advertising or analytics.

## Your control

- You decide when to take notes and where they're saved, and you can delete them like any other file.
- Uninstalling the app removes it. Your notes stay in their folder until you delete them.
